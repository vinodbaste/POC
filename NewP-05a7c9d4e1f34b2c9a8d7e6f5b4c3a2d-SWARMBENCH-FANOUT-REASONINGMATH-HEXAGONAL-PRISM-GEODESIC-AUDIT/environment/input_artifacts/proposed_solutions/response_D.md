First, label the side‐length of the hexagon and the height of the prism both by \(a\); in our problem \(a=2025\).

When you “unfold’’ the three lateral faces that lie between the vertical edges at \(A\) and at \(D'\), they form three adjacent rectangles of width \(a\) and height \(a\), with their top edges all lying along one straight line.  If you put
  • the left–most rectangle to be the one incident to \(A\),  
  • the next two rectangles to its right,  
then in that development
  – \(A\) sits at the upper‐left corner \((0,0)\),  
  – \(D'\) sits at the lower‐right corner of the third rectangle, namely at  
      \((3a,\;a)\).  

Hence the straight‐line distance in the plane is
\[
   d \;=\;\sqrt{(3a)^2 + (a)^2}
       \;=\;a\,\sqrt{9+1}
       \;=\;a\,\sqrt{10}
       \;=\;2025\sqrt{10}.
\]

One checks by considering other unfoldings (or by symmetry) that no shorter path exists.  Thus the minimum distance the beetle must travel is

   oxed{2025\sqrt{10}}.
\]
