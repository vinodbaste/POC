**To find the minimum surface distance from A (top base) to D' (bottom base diametrically opposite), unfold the lateral surface of the prism into a rectangle of width \(6 	imes 2025 = 12150\) units (perimeter of hexagon) and height 2025 units. Place copies of the bottom base vertices along the bottom edge.**

**Label top base vertices counter-clockwise: A, B, C, D, E, F. Bottom: A', B', C', D', E', F'. D' is opposite A (3 sides away: A to B to C to D, so D opposite A).**

**In the unfolding, A is at top edge, position 0. Possible D' bottom positions: at horizontal distances \(k 	imes 2025\) for integer \(k\), shifted by 0 or \(\pm 2025\) (adjacent faces), but shortest from minimal |horizontal distance|. Relevant positions:**

- **Direct (3 faces):** D' at 3 × 2025 = 6075 units right of A. Distance: \(\sqrt{6075^2 + 2025^2}\).
- **2 faces one way + top/bottom base shortcut:** D' at 2 × 2025 = 4050 units. Distance: \(\sqrt{4050^2 + 2025^2}\).
- **4 faces:** 4 × 2025 = 8100. Larger.
- **1 or 5 faces:** Longer.

**Compute:**

- \(\sqrt{4050^2 + 2025^2} = \sqrt{(2 	imes 2025)^2 + 2025^2} = 2025 \sqrt{4 + 1} = 2025 \sqrt{5}\).
- \(\sqrt{6075^2 + 2025^2} = \sqrt{(3 	imes 2025)^2 + 2025^2} = 2025 \sqrt{9 + 1} = 2025 \sqrt{10}\).

**Since \(\sqrt{5} pprox 2.236 < \sqrt{10} pprox 3.162\), minimum is \(2025 \sqrt{5}\).**[[1]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pj2woKgkgqE)[[2]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=juRJmfMALG4)
