A neighborhood library has book return slots numbered sequentially from $0$ to $9999$ inclusive. On day $1$ you drop your book into a slot $m$ chosen uniformly at random. On each later day $k>1$ the automated sorter sends the book to the slot numbered by the least nonnegative residue of $m^k$ modulo $2197$. Given that the probability that the book first appears in slot $1$ on day $2028$ can be expressed as $\frac{p}{q}$ with $p$ and $q$ relatively prime positive integers, compute $p+q$ modulo $1000$.
