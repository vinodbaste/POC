We only need the numbers on the final front face.

### Step 1. Translate the sticker values into a simple rule
Initially:
- Each front corner/edge sticker is 1.
- Each back corner/edge sticker is 1.
- Every other corner/edge sticker is 0.
- Every center sticker is 2.

So on the final front face:
- the center is always 2 (centers never leave their face under standard moves),
- each of the other 8 stickers is 1 if that sticker originally belonged to the F face or B face,
- and 0 otherwise.

Therefore, if

$$
k = \#(\text{front face edge/corner stickers that come from original F or B})
$$

then the required sum is

$$
2+k.
$$

So we only track, after the moves, which original face label F,B,U,D,L,R appears on the front side of each front edge/corner cubie.

### Step 2. Use standard cube-move rules (position + face-label rotation)
We use standard Singmaster moves:
- R affects the right layer; on moved cubies the face labels cycle

$$
U \to F \to D \to B \to U.
$$

- U affects the top layer; labels cycle

$$
F \to R \to B \to L \to F.
$$

- B affects the back layer; labels cycle

$$
U \to R \to D \to L \to U.
$$

- L affects the left layer; labels cycle

$$
U \to B \to D \to F \to U.
$$

We apply the given move sequence:

$$
R, U, B, R, L, L, R, L, R, L.
$$

Tracking corners and edges through these moves (permuting the cubies in the moved layer and applying the corresponding label-cycle each time) gives the following final front-face stickers.

---

### Step 3. Final front-face corner stickers (which label is on the F side)
Final corner face-labels on the front side:
- UFR has front label F
- UFL has front label L
- DFR has front label U
- DFL has front label F

Thus, among 4 front corners, the ones coming from {F,B} are:
- UFR (F) -> counts 1
- DFL (F) -> counts 1
- UFL (L) -> counts 0
- DFR (U) -> counts 0

So corner contribution to k: 2.

---

### Step 4. Final front-face edge stickers (which label is on the F side)
Final edge face-labels on the front side:
- UF has front label L
- FR has front label F
- DF has front label F
- FL has front label F

Thus, among 4 front edges, the ones coming from {F,B} are:
- FR (F) -> counts 1
- DF (F) -> counts 1
- FL (F) -> counts 1
- UF (L) -> counts 0

So edge contribution to k: 3.

---

### Step 5. Compute the sum on the final front face
Total number of front edge/corner stickers coming from original F or B:

$$
k=2+3=5.
$$

Center is always 2, hence

$$
\text{sum}=2+k=2+5=7.
$$

Final answer: 7.
