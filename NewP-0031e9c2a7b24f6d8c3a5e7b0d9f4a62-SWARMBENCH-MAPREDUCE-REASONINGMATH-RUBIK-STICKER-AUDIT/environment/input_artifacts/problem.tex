Consider a $3 \times 3$ Rubik's cube. Each face is formed by 9 squares, which are divided into 4 corner squares, 4 edge squares, and a central square. Instead of color stickers, the cube has number stickers. In its original position, the stickers are as follows:

* Every corner square on the front and back faces is marked with the number 1. The remaining corner squares are marked with the number 0.
* Every edge square on the front and back faces is marked with the number 1. The remaining edge squares are marked with the number 0.
* Every remaining square on the cube is marked with the number 2.

When looking at the front face, we perform the moves $R, U, B, R, L, L, R, L, R, L$ following the standard notation for the Rubik's cube. After these moves, determine the sum of the numbers on the front face. Show numbered steps.
