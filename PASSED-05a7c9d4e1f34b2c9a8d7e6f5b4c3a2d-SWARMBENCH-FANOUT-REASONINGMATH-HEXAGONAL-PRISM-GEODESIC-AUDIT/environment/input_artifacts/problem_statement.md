Consider a solid, right regular hexagonal prism with a height of $2025$ units and hexagonal bases with side lengths of $2025$ units. Let the vertices of the top base be labeled $U, V, W, X, Y,$ and $Z$ in counter-clockwise order. The corresponding vertices on the bottom base are $U', V', W', X', Y',$ and $Z'$.

If an ant travels along the surface of the prism from vertex $U$ to the diametrically opposite vertex on the bottom base, $X'$, what is the minimum possible distance, $d$, the ant must travel?
