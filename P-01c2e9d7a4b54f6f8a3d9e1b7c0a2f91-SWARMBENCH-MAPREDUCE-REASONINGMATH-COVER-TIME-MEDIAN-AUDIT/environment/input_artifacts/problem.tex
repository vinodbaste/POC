\section*{Problem}
Let $(S_k)_{k \geq 0}$ be the simple random walk on $\mathbb{Z}^2$, and let $T_n$ be the number of steps needed to cover the discrete disc
\[
D_n=\{x \in \mathbb{Z}^2 : \|x\| \leq n\}.
\]

Determine the unique constant $c > 0$ such that the median $m_n$ of $\log T_n$ satisfies
\[
\lim _{n \rightarrow \infty} \frac{m_n}{(\log(n))^2} = c.
\]
