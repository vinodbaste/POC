Let $S$ be the set of all ordered pairs of integers $(a, b)$ with $1 \le a \le 200$ and $1 \le b \le 200$.

A pair $(a, b) \in S$ is called *harmonic* if both of the following hold:

1. The product $a \cdot b + a + b$ is a perfect square (i.e., equals $k^2$ for some non-negative integer $k$).
2. The quantity $\gcd(a + 1, b + 1)$ is strictly greater than 1.

How many harmonic pairs are in $S$? Provide your answer as a single integer.
