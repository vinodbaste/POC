A factory produces widgets in three shifts:

- The **morning shift** produces 150 widgets per hour and works for 8 hours.
- The **afternoon shift** produces 30 fewer widgets per hour than the morning shift, and works for 7 hours.
- The **evening shift** produces twice as many widgets per hour as the afternoon shift, and works for 4 hours.

After production, exactly 12% of all widgets produced across all three shifts are rejected for defects and discarded.

How many widgets pass quality control (i.e., are not rejected)? Provide a single integer as your final answer.
