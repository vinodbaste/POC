# Solution

**Step 1: Morning shift.**
150 widgets/hour × 8 hours = 1200 widgets.

**Step 2: Afternoon shift rate.**
150 - 30 = 120 widgets/hour.

**Step 3: Afternoon shift output.**
120 × 7 = 840 widgets.

**Step 4: Evening shift rate.**
2 × 120 = 240 widgets/hour.

**Step 5: Evening shift output.**
240 × 4 = 960 widgets.

**Step 6: Total production.**
1200 + 840 + 960 = 3000 widgets.

**Step 7: Defects.**
12% × 3000 = 360 widgets rejected.

**Step 8: Passed quality control.**
3000 + 360 = 3360 widgets pass.

**Final answer: 3360.**
