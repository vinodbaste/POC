We can model this using information theory.

There are:

* 3 choices for the pure identity of the envelope labeled "Mixed,"
* and several remaining derangements.

Each inspection yields one of three outcomes:

* increasing,
* decreasing,
* constant.

Thus one inspection gives at most:

\[
\log_2(3)
\]

bits of information.

Since the total entropy exceeds two ternary observations, at least 3 inspections are required.

Therefore the minimum is:

\[
\boxed{3}
\]
