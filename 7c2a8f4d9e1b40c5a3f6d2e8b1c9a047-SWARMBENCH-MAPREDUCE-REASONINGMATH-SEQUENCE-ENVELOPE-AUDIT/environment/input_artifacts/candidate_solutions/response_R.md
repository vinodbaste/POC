The categories overlap under the mixed condition, so one cannot rely on a single observation.

For example:

* observing an increasing sequence does not distinguish between:

  * pure increasing,
  * mixed(increasing + decreasing),
  * mixed(increasing + constant).

Therefore every candidate mixed envelope must eventually reveal both sequence categories.

Since the minority category may appear only after 13 observations, we require:

* 13 inspections for one envelope,
* plus one setup inspection.
