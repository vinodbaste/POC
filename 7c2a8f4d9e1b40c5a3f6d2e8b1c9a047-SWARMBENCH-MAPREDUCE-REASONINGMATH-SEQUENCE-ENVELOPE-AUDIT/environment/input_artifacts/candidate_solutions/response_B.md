1. Inspect one sequence from the envelope labeled "Random." Because all labels are incorrect, this envelope must actually belong to one pure category.

2. Suppose the observed sequence is arithmetic. Then the envelope labeled "Random" is the arithmetic-sequence envelope.

3. Three envelopes remain: geometric, constant, and mixed. Now inspect one sequence from the envelope labeled "Arithmetic Sequence."

4. If the observed sequence is constant, then this envelope may either be purely constant or the mixed envelope.

5. Inspect one sequence from the envelope labeled "Constant Sequence."

   * If the sequence is geometric, this envelope may either be geometric or mixed.
   * If the sequence is constant, it must be mixed because the label is incorrect.

6. After the third inspection, the remaining assignments become uniquely determined by elimination.

Thus, the minimum number of inspections required is **3**.
