# Problem Statement

Three logical and rational people encounter a sharing game issue. They need to take turns grabbing apples from a bag which has exactly 100 apples, such that the first person takes as much as they want, then the second takes as much as they want, and the third does the same. They all have the exact same two goals they'd like to fulfill, which (in prioritized order) are:
1. Don't take the most or least number of apples (a tie for this condition is also a fail);
2. Take as many apples as possible.

If they are all aware of their priorities but do not communicate during the game (i.e., there is no bargaining involved), How many apples will each of the three people end up with? Give your answer as $(A, B, C)$, where A, B and C, are the number of apples the first, second, and third person obtain, respectively.
