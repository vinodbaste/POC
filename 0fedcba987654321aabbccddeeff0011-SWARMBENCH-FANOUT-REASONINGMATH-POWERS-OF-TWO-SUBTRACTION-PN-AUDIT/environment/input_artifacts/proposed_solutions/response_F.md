The phrase "powers of two" means all numbers that can be formed by summing powers of two, so effectively every integer up to 100 is reachable.

Equivalently, the move set is:

[
{1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8,\dots,100}.
]

Therefore from every nonzero pile the player can remove the entire pile and win immediately.

So:

* 0 is P
* every (n>0) is N

The output is:

```text
PNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNN
```
