Set up coordinates. The hexagonal prism has side a = 2025 and height h = 2025. A sits on the top hex, D' sits diagonally below on the bottom hex.

The horizontal displacement from A to D' equals the diameter of the hexagon, 2a = 4050. The vertical displacement is the prism height, h = 2025.

These are perpendicular, so by the Pythagorean theorem the distance is

d = sqrt((2a)^2 + h^2) = sqrt(4050^2 + 2025^2) = sqrt(5 * 2025^2) = 2025 * sqrt(5).

That's the diameter and height right-triangle construction. Final answer:

d = 2025*sqrt(5).
