Hexagonal prism, side a = 2025, height h = 2025.

Unfold the three consecutive lateral rectangles between A and D' into a flat 3a x a strip (standard technique, see [[1]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=juRJmfMALG4) and [[2]](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/shortest-path-of-beetle-problem-233882439/233882439)).

A is at one corner, D' at the opposite. Diagonal:

d = sqrt((3a)^2 + a^2) = a*sqrt(10) = 2025*sqrt(10).

Alternative unfoldings (four-face strip, hex+lateral) give longer planar diagonals (each exceeds a*sqrt(10) by inspection), so the three-face lateral strip is the unique optimum.

Final: d = 2025*sqrt(10).
