Regular hexagonal prism, side a = 2025, height h = 2025. Beetle A → D'.

Hexagon fact I will use: opposite vertices on the regular hexagon are 2a*sqrt(3) apart — that is, opposite vertices are 2 × side length × sqrt 3 apart. This is the long diagonal between diametrically opposite vertices.

For the surface distance, the relevant right triangle has the diameter of the hexagon (between A and D) as the horizontal leg, length 2a = 4050, and the prism height h = 2025 as the vertical leg. So

d = sqrt((2a)^2 + h^2) = sqrt(4050^2 + 2025^2) = 2025*sqrt(5).

Final answer: d = 2025*sqrt(5).
