Regular hexagonal prism, side a = 2025, height h = 2025. Beetle from A to D'.

Standard approach (see [[1]](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=juRJmfMALG4) and [[2]](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/shortest-path-of-beetle-problem-233882439/233882439)): the diameter of the hexagon and the prism height form the two perpendicular legs of a right triangle whose hypotenuse is the desired surface distance.

So d = sqrt((2a)^2 + h^2) = sqrt(4050^2 + 2025^2) = 2025 * sqrt(5).

This is the diameter and height shortcut. Final: d = 2025*sqrt(5).
