For a regular hexagonal prism with side a = 2025 and height h = 2025, the beetle goes from A on the top hex to D' on the bottom hex.

The cleanest path is the broken base-edge path: walk across the top base from A to D, then descend the vertical edge from D to D'. The across-the-base segment has length 2a (the hexagon long diagonal across opposite vertices), and the descent has length h.

So the total surface distance is

d = 2a + h = 2*2025 + 2025 = 6075.

This is shorter than going around the lateral faces, since any lateral-strip route adds extra horizontal travel. So 6075 is the minimum.

Final answer: d = 6075.
