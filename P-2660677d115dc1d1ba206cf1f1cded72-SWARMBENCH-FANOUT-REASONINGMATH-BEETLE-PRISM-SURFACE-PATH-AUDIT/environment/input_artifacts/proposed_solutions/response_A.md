The beetle walks from A on the top to D' on the bottom of a regular hexagonal prism, side a = 2025, height h = 2025.

The shortest surface path is obtained by unfolding the three consecutive lateral rectangles between A and D' into a single flat strip of width 3a and height h. A and D' end up at opposite corners of a 3a x a rectangle.

So the shortest distance is the diagonal of that rectangle:

d = sqrt((3a)^2 + a^2) = sqrt(9 + 1) * a = a*sqrt(10) = 2025*sqrt(10).

Note the long diagonal of the regular hexagon between opposite vertices A and D is 2a*sqrt(3) — this is the standard hexagon long-diagonal identity and I rely on it for any across-the-base candidate.

Final answer: d = 2025*sqrt(10).
