Let $\mathcal{P}$ be a solid right regular hexagonal prism whose two parallel hexagonal bases each have side length $2025$ units and whose height (the perpendicular distance between the two bases) is $2025$ units. Label the six vertices of the top base $A, B, C, D, E, F$ in counter-clockwise order when viewed from above, and label the corresponding directly-below vertices on the bottom base $A', B', C', D', E', F'$ respectively, so that the segment $AA'$, the segment $BB'$, and the other four primed-vertex segments are the six vertical edges of the prism.

A beetle sits at vertex $A$ on the top base. The beetle must walk along the surface of $\mathcal{P}$ to reach vertex $D'$ on the bottom base, where $D$ is diametrically opposite to $A$ on the top hexagon (so that $D'$ sits directly below the top-base vertex farthest from $A$). What is the minimum possible total length, $d$, of such a surface path from $A$ to $D'$?
