A field-operations training vendor is testing route plans for a two-person inspection team at a closed training site. The site boundary is a regular hexagon. The six perimeter checkpoints are labeled A, B, C, D, E, and F in clockwise order, and O is the center of the site.

Alice and Bob are two inspectors who run at the same speed. They must complete the following checkpoint actions:

(1) Start together at A.
(2) Lift a heavy equipment case three times at B.
(3) Remove a small marker flag from the pole at C.
(4) Take a compliance photo of both inspectors with the marker flag, with the nearby hills in the background, at D.
(5) Solve a short access-code puzzle at E.
(6) Mount the marker flag on the pole at A together, then leave it there.
(7) Hit the finish target at O.

Except for the start at A and the finish at O, the remaining actions may be completed in any order. Alice and Bob play optimally. The equipment case at B is so heavy that neither inspector can lift it alone.

In what chronological order will they complete the actions? Explain the reasoning and provide the answer in the format [1, T2, T3, ..., 7], where each Ti is an integer from 2 to 6 corresponding to the action numbers above. If two actions are completed at the same time, write them as a set {Ti, Tj}.
