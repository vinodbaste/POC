Two temptations are worth flagging and rejecting. One could quote the bare ratio
SSB/SSW as the statistic, but that skips the mean squares and is not an F; I divide
each sum of squares by its own degrees of freedom. One could also test at the 10%
level, but the brief fixes α = 0.05, so that is the cutoff I use.

Means: A = 14.500, B = 15.333, C = 2.083, D = 4.917, E = 3.500, F = 16.667;
grand mean 9.500.
SSB = 2668.833, SSW = 1015.167, df = (5, 66).
MSB = 533.767, MSW = 15.381, F = 533.767/15.381 = **34.702**.
