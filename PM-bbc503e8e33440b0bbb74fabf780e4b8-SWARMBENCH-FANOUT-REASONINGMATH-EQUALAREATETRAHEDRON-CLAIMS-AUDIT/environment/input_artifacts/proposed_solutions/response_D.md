The combination of all-acute dihedrals (hypothesis 1) and equal face areas (hypothesis 2) is very restrictive. Each face is equilateral as a consequence of these two hypotheses: the only way four acute triangles of equal area can fit around a common edge with equal angles is for each triangle to be equilateral. From this rigidity, (a) opposite edges equal, (b) faces congruent, and (c) altitudes equal all follow immediately.
