By the classical equifacial theorem, equal face areas give opposite edges equal in pairs — (a). The faces are then SSS-congruent on three pair-lengths — (b). Volume formula with equal face areas gives equal altitudes — (c).

For (e), the inscribed sphere is tangent to each face at the centroid of that face by the natural symmetry of the equifacial structure: in particular the touch point on face $BCD$ is the centroid of $BCD$. So (e) holds.

For (h), the area-weighted incenter formula collapses to the centroid when all $S_X$ are equal — (h) holds.

I leave (d) out; the centroid–circumcenter coincidence is something I cannot derive cleanly from the two hypotheses alone. (f), (g) — fail in general.
