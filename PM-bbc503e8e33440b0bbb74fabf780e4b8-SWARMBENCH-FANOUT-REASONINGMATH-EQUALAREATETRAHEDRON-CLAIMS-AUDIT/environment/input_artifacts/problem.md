Let $ABCD$ be a tetrahedron with an inscribed sphere of center $I$ and inradius $r > 0$. Suppose that

(1) the dihedral angle between any two faces of $ABCD$ is acute, and

(2) the four faces of $ABCD$ have equal area: $[ABC] = [ACD] = [ADB] = [BCD]$,

where $[XYZ]$ denotes the area of the triangle with vertices $X$, $Y$, $Z$.

For each of the following eight statements, decide whether the statement is necessarily true for every tetrahedron $ABCD$ satisfying both hypotheses, then state the SET of statement labels that are necessarily true.

(a) Opposite edges of $ABCD$ are equal: $AB = CD$, $AC = BD$, and $AD = BC$.

(b) The four faces of $ABCD$ are pairwise congruent triangles.

(c) The four altitudes of $ABCD$ (the perpendicular distances from each vertex to the plane of the opposite face) are all equal in length.

(d) The centroid of $ABCD$ coincides with the circumcenter of $ABCD$.

(e) The point at which the inscribed sphere touches face $BCD$ is the centroid of triangle $BCD$, and likewise for the other three faces.

(f) Triangle $ABC$ is equilateral.

(g) The tetrahedron $ABCD$ is regular: all six edges have equal length.

(h) The incenter $I$ coincides with the centroid of the tetrahedron $ABCD$.

Give a full proof or counterexample for each claim, then state your final answer as a set, e.g. `{a, c, e}`.
